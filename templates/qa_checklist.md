# Review checklist — [shot or full short]

## Story / factual accuracy
- [ ] The image supports the line being spoken.
- [ ] Cause and consequence are shown in the right order.
- [ ] Unverified details are removed or qualified.

## Frame-by-frame / motion
- [ ] Review the shot at 30 fps or inspect a sequential 30 fps sheet.
- [ ] No one-frame pops, flicker, duplicated freeze frames, geometry changes, or unwanted cuts.
- [ ] Camera path is smooth with deliberate easing; parallax matches the 3D layout.
- [ ] Character weight, feet, hands, and prop contact remain physically connected.

## Image
- [ ] Face, wardrobe, props, light direction, and set match the continuity bible.
- [ ] Subject is separated from the background and readable at phone size.
- [ ] Shadows/reflections agree with lights and contact.
- [ ] Texture scale fits the shot; no plastic skin or uniformly noisy detail.
- [ ] Grade and exposure match the selected profile.
- [ ] Captions are accurate, timed, and do not cover key action.

## Sound / export
- [ ] Voice remains intelligible under music and effects.
- [ ] Music/SFX align to story beats.
- [ ] Export duration, aspect ratio, frame rate, and audio sync are correct.
