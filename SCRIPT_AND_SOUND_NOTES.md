# Script, captions, and sound notes

## Evidence available

The short clips have burned-in English captions. The notes in `references/clip_notes/` reconstruct their visible narrative beats and cite readable caption fragments where useful. This is **not a verified, word-for-word transcript of the spoken audio**. The supplied long transcript is the YouTube tutorial describing how its creator made a different hybrid 2D-character/3D-world short; its method is summarized in `METHOD_FROM_TUTORIAL.md`.

No claims are made here about the exact narrator delivery, pitch, breaths, music, or sound effects because those require an audio-listening/transcription pass. Caption timing is approximate and can trail or lead the spoken words.

## Cross-video writing pattern

- Lead with the central visual contradiction, danger, or surprising setup.
- Keep the narration concrete: named object, action, consequence.
- Let visual change carry a sentence transition (environment changes, a prop is revealed, a character tries something, a timeline compresses).
- Add a pivot or complication around the midpoint, then a specific payoff.
- Avoid repeating a shot while the narration moves to a different fact.
- Use the final image to echo the hook or make the cost/result visible.
