## Craft observations

- **Lighting/grade:** high-key, stable, diffuse and friendly. Mean luma is about 0.510; mean saturation about 0.303. Avoid cinematic darkness or thriller contrast here.
- **Depth:** clear classroom layout matters more than shallow focus. Keep the back corner visible while the narration defines the rule. Students, desks, and teacher establish the social setting.
- **Camera:** modest pushes/pans and one quick reframe/whip-like move supply energy. Timing and geography matter more than elaborate camera paths.
- **Texture/outline:** smooth stylized geometry, blue brick/wall treatment, carpet, wood desks, and bright sign. No strong black ink contour style. Keep facial features readable at the source's small 360×640 resolution.
- **Expression/realism:** simplified, slightly doll-like student faces; understated reaction is part of the joke. Give gestures clear poses, but avoid uncanny over-acting.
- **Best transferable idea:** an unusual rule can be the visual payoff, and a prop/sign can make the final joke understandable without an extra explanation.

## Risks to watch in a new version

Keep the boy, teacher, room layout, and sign locations fixed. The green/yellow gas effect should be brief, tied to the boy's action, and physically integrated rather than floating as a detached cloud. Preserve the joke's nonreaction beat. Do not crowd subtitles over the sign or students' faces.
